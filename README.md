# The Makar-Limanov Invariant Equals Its Slice Version in Dimension Three

Deep Bhattacharjee.

Over any field of characteristic zero, the Makar-Limanov invariant of a domain coincides with its slice version whenever the latter has transcendence degree at most one or one less than that of the domain. In dimension three this settles a question of Dasgupta and Gupta.
